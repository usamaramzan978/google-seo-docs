# Opt out of display on local search results and other Google properties

Website owners can prevent their crawled content from appearing across several Google services
by using the opt-out feature in Search Console.

## Covered Google properties

The opt-out applies to:

- Google Shopping
- Google Flights
- Google Hotels and vacation rentals
- Local search results

## Key details

**Removal timeline**: Content currently being displayed on any of these properties will be
removed within 30 days of opting out.

**Domain-level application**: The opt-out operates at the domain level. Owners can opt out entire
domains including subdomains (e.g., `example.com` covers `sub.example.com`), but cannot
selectively opt out individual subdomains or directories. Separate domains require individual
opt-out requests.

**Geographic scope**: For local search results, the opt-out applies globally. For other covered
properties, it applies only to google.com domain services.

## Access

[View or change your opt-out setting in Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/opt-out).
